05 / A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before the
first coat.

Every space is different.
Here’s a good place to start.

What is the difference between Gamazine and Glamourcotes?

Both are decorative wall finishes, with textures and appearance that vary by product and application. A physical sample is the best way to compare the options in your own lighting. We can discuss the look you want before selecting a finish.

Can you work on interior and exterior walls?

Our services cover interior walls, exterior walls, boundary walls, ceilings and epoxy floors. The coating and preparation must suit the surface and its exposure, so suitability is confirmed as part of the assessment.

Do walls need preparation first?

Yes. The existing coating, cracks, loose material and any damp areas need to be assessed before application. The required repairs and preparation form part of the agreed scope; a decorative coating is not a substitute for resolving underlying problems.

How do I choose a colour or texture?

Save a gallery reference or describe the style you like. View a physical sample in your room or outside light before deciding, because screen colours and design reference images cannot show an exact product match.

Can I choose the colour and pattern of an epoxy floor?

Yes. Explore metallic, marble-effect and flowing colour designs in our gallery, then discuss a sample and the look you want. We’ll assess the existing floor and agree the preparation, finish and application schedule before work begins.

What should I include in a quote request?

Include your suburb or town, the type of surface, approximate size if you know it, its current condition and your preferred finish. You can add photographs directly in WhatsApp after opening the conversation.

How much will it cost, and how long will it take?

That depends on the area, surface condition, access, preparation and chosen finish. A price and schedule can be agreed once the project details have been reviewed. There are no fixed estimates on this website.
